# The MGL loads two shells at a time from the pouch

Order: 12000
From: the ideas repo, `games/re3.md` (<https://github.com/TefMeister/mod-ideas/blob/main/games/re3.md>), copied 2026-11-01

`[raw]` · `[looks doable]` — *judged from a recon of the gun only, not from a build (2026-11-01)*

**What Tefa said (2026-11-01, in session):** LG at the pouch gives **two** grenade shells: one in the palm, one between the fingers. After the first goes in, the hand
animates so the second sits between the fingers; LG + LT puts them in. Possibly: with this weapon only, LG held at the
pouch "opens" the chambers for loading, and they close again when LG is let go. Tefa wants to see in game first how the
MGL's own reload puts rounds in.
**What it'd take:** the MGL into Visceral RE3's single-round loading (as RE2's GM 79 now is), a second round shown in the
hand, a finger pose for each. Recon 2026-11-01: the MGL model has no hinge; the game's reload turns the cylinder (`_01`,
100 deg about its length) and moves the rounds `_06`..`_09`, `_101` `[measured 2026-11-01, simulator]`.
⚠️ "looks doable" is judged from that recon only, NOT from a build: the two-shells-in-one-hand pose and showing a second
round are untried.
